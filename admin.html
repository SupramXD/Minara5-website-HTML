Connecting Nedbank

Now: download a CSV statement from Nedbank Online Banking (Money Edge / Business Online) and import it under Nedbank & Payouts. This is the fastest, zero-credential option and works with every Nedbank business account.

Later: Nedbank does not offer a public self-service transaction API. A live feed needs a Nedbank-approved aggregator / Open Banking provider (e.g. Stitch, Ozow, or a Money Edge integration arranged through your banker) with business credentials. The CSV ledger here is already shaped for that feed — only the server-side connector would need to be added.

Only the master admin can read these collections (Firestore rules are admin-only). Cost prices are never written to the public products.json.
